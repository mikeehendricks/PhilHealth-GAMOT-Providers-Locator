      What is the PhilHealth Yakap & GAMOT Program?

      Yakap GAMOT Locator helps you find the nearest PhilHealth Yakap and GAMOT (Guaranteed and Accessible Medications for Outpatient Treatment) accredited providers anywhere in the Philippines. Allow GPS access and tap Locate Me to see the providers nearest you, or search by provider name, city, municipality, or province to locate accredited pharmacies, hospitals, and rural health units that dispense free medicines under PhilHealth.

      About the GAMOT Benefit Package

      GAMOT — Guaranteed and Accessible Medications for Outpatient Treatment — is PhilHealth's outpatient drug benefit. It provides eligible members with free access to 75 essential medicines, with an annual benefit limit of up to ₱20,000 per beneficiary. It is a component of the PhilHealth YAKAP (Yaman ng Kalusugan) program.

      Who can avail of the GAMOT benefit?

      • PhilHealth members and their dependents
      • Patients with a GAMOT prescription from a YAKAP-accredited doctor
      • Prescriptions bearing a Unique Prescription Security Code (UPSC)

      How to claim free medicines

      1. Register in the PhilHealth YAKAP program via eGovPH.
      2. Consult a YAKAP-accredited doctor.
      3. Receive a GAMOT prescription with a Unique Prescription Security Code (UPSC).
      4. Present the prescription and a valid ID at an accredited GAMOT provider.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      What is PhilHealth GAMOT?

      GAMOT (Guaranteed and Accessible Medications for Outpatient Treatment) is PhilHealth's outpatient drug benefit that provides free access to 75 essential medicines for eligible members.

      What is PhilHealth Yakap?

      YAKAP (Yaman ng Kalusugan) is PhilHealth's comprehensive primary care program. The GAMOT medicines benefit is delivered through YAKAP-accredited providers.

      How do I find the nearest GAMOT provider?

      Use the map above: allow GPS and tap Locate Me, or search by city or province, then tap any provider for turn-by-turn directions.

      Are the medicines really free?

      Yes. Eligible PhilHealth members can get covered medicines at no cost from accredited GAMOT providers, up to the ₱20,000 annual benefit limit per beneficiary.

      What is a UPSC?

      A Unique Prescription Security Code (UPSC) is a code on a GAMOT prescription issued by a YAKAP-accredited doctor, which an accredited provider uses to validate and dispense your free medicines.
